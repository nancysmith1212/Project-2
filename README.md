# CE 321 Project 2 Grader

## Setup

Log into your GitHub account, then click "Use this template" in the upper-right corner of this page to make your own copy of this repository.

## Submitting your work

1. Edit `project_2_submission.txt` to enter your Project 2 results.
2. Upload your hand-edited CSV files into the `CSV_Files` folder. They must keep these exact names:
   - `Gabled_Howe_Six_Panel.csv`
   - `Gabled_Howe_Eight_Panel.csv`
   - `Scissor_Truss_Eight_Panel.csv`
3. Upload your `Geometry_Operations.py`, `Structure_Operations.py`, and `Method_of_Joints.py` files to the main folder of the repository, replacing the starter files.

Do not edit, move, or delete the `CSV_Files/DO_NOT_EDIT` folder. The grader needs it.

Test your code on your own machine before uploading. Code that runs forever (for example, an infinite loop) will stop the grader before it reports a score.

## Seeing your score

Click the Actions tab and open the most recent run. A red X means at least one test did not pass. Open the "Project 2 Grader Code" step to see your score and comments for each question.

When you are satisfied with your score, also submit your files on Learning Suite to receive full credit.

Please let me know if any of these tests are not behaving as you would anticipate.
